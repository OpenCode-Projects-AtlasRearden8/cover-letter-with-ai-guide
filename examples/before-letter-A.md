# Example Letter — Before (generic AI output, cold prompt)

**Fictional composite.** This is what a chatbot produces when you type
"Write me a cover letter for a Customer Success Manager role at HealthSync, I'm a nurse."

---

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to express my strong interest in the Customer Success Manager position at HealthSync. With my extensive background in healthcare, I am confident that I possess the unique blend of skills necessary to excel in this dynamic role.

Throughout my career, I have consistently demonstrated exceptional communication skills, strong attention to detail, and a proven ability to lead cross-functional teams. I am passionate about leveraging technology to improve patient outcomes and believe I would be a valuable asset to your innovative team.

I am drawn to HealthSync's forward-thinking culture and would welcome the opportunity to contribute to your continued success. I am available at your convenience for an interview and look forward to discussing how my skills align with your needs.

Sincerely,
Sam

---

## Autopsy (Chapter 4's checklist applied)

| Problem | Where it shows |
|---|---|
| Zero facts about the writer | Every sentence — nothing here couldn't be sent by anyone |
| Vague enthusiasm stacking | "strong interest" / "dynamic role" / "innovative team" |
| Resume re-run instead of an argument | Paragraph 2 lists golden traits, no evidence |
| Flattery without reading | "forward-thinking culture" was never checked |
| No number, project, or event anywhere | Whole letter |
| Reads like a form letter | Because it is one |
