# Example Prompt Pack — Chapter 3 Recipe 3, filled in (sanitized)

**Fictional composite.** This is the exact prompt Sam R. pasted into a local
Ollama model to produce the "After" letter. Notice: employer names swapped for
neutral descriptions, no dates that resolve to identity, no patient details, no
colleague names — Chapter 6 applied before anything was ever typed anywhere.

---

```
Draft a 200-word cover letter. One argument: "I am the product's end
user already." Neutralize all employer names as generic descriptions.
Plain sentences, not resume-speak. If any fact below doesn't clearly
support the argument, leave it out and list it at the end. After the
draft, flag any sentence where you used a word stronger than the
facts support.

FACTS (sanitized proof bank):
- 8 years RN: 6 med-surg, 2 PACU
- charge nurse ~2 shifts/week, last 3 years, 32-bed unit
- volunteered to pilot smart infusion pump adoption; trained 20
  night-shift nurses on it
- built a shift-change handoff checklist that charge nurses kept
  using after I rotated off
- 8 years of 12-hour shifts, zero call-offs
- precepted 5 new grads

ROLE (sanitized posting extract):
Title: Customer Success Manager, nurse-scheduling platform
Repeated wants: building relationships with clinical users;
training and adoption; reducing churn at hospitals;
understanding nurse workflows
```

---

## What came back, and what got cut

- The first draft claimed "led patient-experience initiatives" — no such fact
  above, so it was deleted (exaggeration guard, Chapter 3 Rule 3).
- The first draft said "expert in workflow design" — softened to "I know what it
  takes to make a process change stick."
- The zero-call-off line was Sam's own addition in the voice pass; "proven
  reliability" was the AI's weaker, vaguer version.
- Hooks came from Recipe 2 (five candidates), then one was rewritten in Sam's
  own voice per Recipe 5.
