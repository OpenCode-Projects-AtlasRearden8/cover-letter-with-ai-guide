What this manual is

A plain-English guide to writing job cover letters with free AI help — whether that AI runs in a web chat or locally on your own computer. It explains how to structure a letter that reads like a person wrote it, how to tailor one letter per job posting without exaggerating, how to prompt AI to pull real proof from your own resume, and how to keep the whole thing ATS-safe.

Everything here is written the way Dylan Asdale (The Massive AI Manual, dylanasdale.github.io) explains AI: like a friend would. Step by step. No jargon walls. Never condescending.

Who it is for

Anyone applying for jobs, and especially healthcare workers pivoting to nonclinical roles. The worked examples follow a fictional-but-realistic nurse, "Sam," moving from bedside care to a remote clinical-liaison role. If you are not a nurse, the structure and prompts work exactly the same.

How to use this manual

Read the chapters in order the first time. After that, it is a reference:

  1. Chapter 1 — The letter structure that reads human (and the structure hiring managers skim for).
  2. Chapter 2 — Tailoring one letter per posting without exaggerating or lying.
  3. Chapter 3 — Prompt recipes that pull proof from your own resume, not invented accomplishments.
  4. Chapter 4 — Nurse-to-nonclinical pivot examples, before and after.
  5. Chapter 5 — ATS-safe formatting: what a robot reads before a human does.
  6. Chapter 6 — The privacy rule: never paste employer names or personal data into cloud tools. Use Ollama locally instead.

There is also docs/templates/ with copy-paste skeletons, and site/ — a static site version of this manual with full before-and-after letter examples you can read side by side.

The three rules worth remembering

  1. The AI drafts; you decide. Every sentence must be true about you and sound like you.
  2. One posting, one letter. Mass-applied identical letters read identical, and hiring managers can tell.
  3. Names out, meaning in. Strip employer names, dates, and personal details before anything leaves your computer. Chapter 6 shows the swap technique.

What this manual is not

It is not a resume checker (see the separate ats-resume-checker-cli project) and not interview coaching (see free-interview-prep-pack). This is strictly the cover letter.

It is also not legal or career advice, and never medical advice. It is education.
