Fictional composite. Same analyst-floor story, every claim resized to exactly what Sam's proof bank can defend, gap stated on purpose.

Dear Hiring Team,

I'm applying for the Clinical Data Analyst posting. My most concrete qualification is modest but real: I built my unit's handoff and safety-trend reports in Excel, and charge nurses kept using them after I rotated off. Six years of daily clinical documentation work also means I know what those data fields actually contain — and, more usefully, what colleagues actually type into them — which I suspect matters for the data-quality questions no dashboard can fix by itself.

I'm not a trained analyst, and I'd rather say that plainly than have it discovered in an interview. What I offer instead is fluency in where this kind of data comes from, honest comfort with spreadsheets, and a demonstrated habit of turning a messy floor problem into a simple report that people actually use.

If that fits what you need for the first year, I'd be glad to talk. Thank you for reading.

Sam R.
