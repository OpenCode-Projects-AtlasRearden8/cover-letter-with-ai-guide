Sample Proof Bank (fictional composite — Sam R.)

This is the raw material Chapter 3 tells you to write once and reuse forever. Everything below is fictional but written the way a real proof bank should look: rough, factual, with real numbers where they exist, no polish.

  • 8 years total RN: 6 med-surg, 2 PACU
  • charge nurse ~2 shifts/week, last 3 years, 32-bed unit
  • volunteered to pilot smart infusion pump rollout; trained 20 night-shift RNs
  • built shift-change handoff checklist in Word; charge nurses kept using it after I rotated off
  • ran unit's fall-prevention huddle for a quarter; falls 9/month → 4/month (small sample — discuss in person, don't letter-cluster this number)
  • precepted 5 new grads, all retained past year one
  • Epic user since go-live at former employer; superuser for order-entry module (user-group role, not vendor certification)
  • made monthly safety-trend reports in Excel for unit council
  • de-escalation training completed; handled escalations with families at least weekly for years
  • 8 years of 12-hour shifts, zero call-offs

How it gets used

  1. Paste the sanitized version (Chapter 6 swap table applied) into your local

AI tool or a chat.

  1. Run Chapter 3 Recipe 3 (the one-argument draft prompt).
  2. The AI works only from these facts. Any number in its draft must trace back

to a line above — otherwise cut it.
